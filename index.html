      FAQ

      Quick answers

      What is Face Audit?

      A free satirical face-roast page. You capture or upload a selfie and get a shareable score card.

      Does my photo leave this device?

      No. Capture, scoring, and card export stay in the browser. Nothing is uploaded.

      Is this medical or scientific?

      No. Not medical advice, not a diagnosis, not a beauty standard. Satire only.

      Is it free?

      Yes. No account. No paywall.

      What is The Calipers?

      A local pin list of roast cards. Pins stay in this browser. No photos are stored, only the score snapshot.

      Does it measure my face?

      No. The score is a seeded random roast. If this browser can see a face box, crop size and position are shown as photo geometry. That is not bone, not medical, not a beauty score.

      This is pure satire and entertainment. Real facial aesthetics are far more complex and subjective. Not medical advice. Not a diagnosis. Stochastic calipers, not science. Touch grass.
